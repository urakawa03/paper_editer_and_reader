## Abstract
A simple derivation of a generalized gradient approximation (GGA) for the exchange-correlation energy is presented, in which all parameters are fundamental constants. The functional, now widely known as PBE, satisfies key exact conditions while remaining simple, and improves upon the local spin density approximation for atomization energies and other properties.
